About

A little more
than the code.

I'm Dimitris, a developer who enjoys building both websites and complete software systems.

I like understanding how everything fits together: what someone sees, what happens behind the scenes, and how the two connect. Working across those layers keeps me curious and gives me new things to figure out.

1 / HOW I THINK

The whole picture

I'm interested in how the interface, the logic, and the data work together. Understanding those connections is part of what I enjoy about development.

2 / WHAT I ENJOY

Something to figure out

I enjoy work with more moving parts: breaking a bigger problem down, making sense of the pieces, and bringing them together.

3 / WHAT KEEPS ME CURIOUS

Going a little deeper

There's always more to learn, whether that means trying a new tool or digging into a topic beyond the code. That's also what excites me about the thesis ahead.
